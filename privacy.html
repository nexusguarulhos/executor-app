Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

executor is a private tool used by one person, its owner. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

Who can use it

Only the owner. There are no other users and no sign-up.

What data it accesses

After the owner signs in with Google and gives consent, executor can access that account's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Tasks, Contacts, Meet and Photos data, within the permissions shown on the consent screen. It accesses data only when the owner, or an assistant acting on his instruction, asks for it.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out what the owner asks, such as reading a message, creating a calendar event or finding a file. It is not used for advertising, profiling or training of general AI models, and it is not sold.

Where it is stored

The Google access tokens are stored encrypted on a server in the owner's home. That server is not reachable from the internet. executor does not keep its own copy of mail, calendar or file contents.

Sharing

Data is not shared with third parties. When the owner uses an AI assistant, the parts of his own data needed for a request are sent to the provider of that assistant to answer that request.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing the connection in executor deletes the stored tokens.
